We're planning a series of friend events during the week leading up to and after the wedding — a chance to explore Taiwan together and spend time in a more relaxed setting. Caleb and Tammy will likely be at most, but maybe not all, of the events, as our friends and family have graciously volunteered to help us host some of these activities.

If you're planning on joining a social event, please message Caleb to be added to the Signal group chat. Signal is a popular end-to-end encrypted chat app similar to Whatsapp or Line.

These events are optional!

A group will begin a mountain trip across Taiwan. We'll start by taking the HSR from Taipei to Taichung, arriving around 12pm and renting a car. Then we'll drive to Lugu, hanging around the area and staying in this hotel for the night.

We'll drive to the mountain town of Ren'Ai, a tea farming area at high altitude with lots of tea shops, hikes, and great views. We'll be staying two nights in a tea farm hotel.

We'll drive from Ren'Ai through Taroko Gorge, stopping here and there to hike or explore. We'll drop the car off in Hualien and stay there the night.
