Friday's activity

Bouquet-making and Picnic

Time

Friday, October 16 at 2:00 PM

Location

Da'an Park, Taipei

About the picnic

Da'an park is the central park of Taipei. We'll spend an afternoon here relaxing, having a picnic, and making bouquets for those interested. For those not interested, we could chuck around a frisbee.

Please grab a snack of some kind to bring, such as something from 7-11. Don't stress about it if you can't, just come anyway.

Exact location in the park TBD, though it will probably be somewhere towards the middle south-east portion of the park, in a gazebo.

We'll be here until probably 5 PM or 6 PM.

This event is open invite. Bring whoever without DMing us to ask about it.

The easiest way to get to Da'an park is to go directly to Da'an Park MRT Station.
