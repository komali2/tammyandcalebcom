Wednesday's activity

Riverside Bike Ride

Time

Wednesday, October 14 at 11:00 AM

Location

Starting and finishing at Gongguan Riverside Plaza.

About the ride

Starting from Gongguan riverside plaza, we'll rent bikes from a local bike rental company right there in the plaza, which should be quite cheap (probably under 10$ USD). The rental place closes for a few hours at 12pm, so it's important to arrive on time. The fastest way to get to the plaza is probably to take the MRT to Gongguan MRT Station, and then walk 10 minutes from there.

We will bicycle over the course of a couple hours to Bitan Scenic Area, near Xindian MRT station, stopping along the way as we please. We'll hang at Bitan for some amount of time, and then bicycle back to Gongguan to return the bikes around 3 PM or 4 PM.

The bicycle ride will be slow and casual.

If it's raining heavily, we'll probably cancel this activity.
