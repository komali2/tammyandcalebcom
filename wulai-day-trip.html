Wulai Day Trip

Time

Thursday, October 15 at 11:00 AM

Location

Wulai Public Hot Springs

About the day trip

Wulai public hot springs are a collection of free riverside hotsprings in Wulai. The activity involves relaxing in (clothed) outdoor public hot springs, and swimming in the river.

There's many ways to get to Wulai. The main group will be meeting at Xindian MRT station at 11 AM. We'll then ride the 849 bus to Wulai, which leaves every 20 minutes. We'll then walk about 15 minutes from Wulai parking lot to the hot spring location, linked above. Note that the link is often deleted due to mass reporting from local hotel owners that don't like to compete with the free hot spring, so if the link is broken, please let Caleb know and he'll get you a new one.

We'll swim for 2-3 hours, and then either hike to Wulai Falls and get a coffee if we have energy, or have an early dinner at my favorite restaurant on Wulai old street.

If it's raining, we'll probably still go to Wulai, but instead of swimming, go straight to the waterfall, hiking with umbrellas, and spend some time at my friend's cafe, or my other friend's cafe if we're too lazy to hike.

If it's raining too heavily, we'll probably skip this activity.

Regardless, we'll take the bus back to Xindian station probably around 5 PM, arriving around 5:45 PM.

This event is open invite. Bring whoever without DMing us to ask about it.
