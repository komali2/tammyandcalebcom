Sunday's activity

Public Hot Spring

Time

Sunday, October 18 at 12:00 PM

Location

Spring City Resort, Beitou, Taipei

About the hot spring

This event involves relaxing in a clothed public artificial outdoor hot spring. The vibe is sort of like a small water park without rides but instead many hot tubs.

The hot spring has certain rules about clothing, such as a swim camp requirement, however they rent this out if you need one. I don't believe they rent out swimsuits though, so bring your swimsuit.

We plan to arrive at 12 PM at the hotel front desk to get tickets. You can arrive whenever, just go to the hotel front desk to get yourself a ticket, which is roughly 20$ USD if I remember correctly.

We'll relax in the hot spring for several hours, and then most likely try to get Hot Pot somewhere after, probably 5 PM or 6 PM, location TBD.

The easiest way to get to Spring City Resort is to show the hotel name in Chinese to a taxi driver at Beitou MRT station, although there are some bus options.

This event is open invite, feel free to bring whoever without DMing us to ask about it.
