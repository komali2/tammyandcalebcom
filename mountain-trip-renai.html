About this leg of the trip

Starting in Lugu, we'll be driving cars / motorcycles slowly to Ren'Ai, also known as Wuling.

We'll be stopping at our leisure, and are staying at a tea farm.

We're staying two nights in Ren'Ai. On the second day, we may drive to a secret riverside hot spring, or find other hikes to do.
