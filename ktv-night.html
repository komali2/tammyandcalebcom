KTV Night

Time

Monday, October 19 at 8:00 PM

Location

Oncor, Xinyi District, Taipei

About KTV night

KTV means Karaoke in a private room with your friends rather than in a public bar. In Taiwan, KTV places often have pretty good food, both meals and snacks, and is often a place to drink. Singing is of course optional, if you don't like to sing, it would probably still be fun to come if you treat it like a bar hangout with people.

We'll be arriving at Oncor at 8 PM. You can arrive later if you want, just ask the people at the front for Tammy, or show them these characters 許婉婷, and they'll take you to our room.

We'll be at Oncor until probably 11 PM, at which point we may go find a late night snack somewhere, or if adventerous, hike Xiangshan / Elephant Mountain.

This event is semi-open invite. All wedding guests are invited, but if you want to bring someone that wasn't a wedding guest, please ask us first so we can ensure space.

Oncor is a little pricey, so if you come, it'd be great if you could chip in 😅
